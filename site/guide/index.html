Shear Budget™ User Guide & Reference

Proactive cash-flow clarity without surprises. Shear Budget answers one definitive question every single day: "Can I cover this right now without risking an unpaid bill or dipping into savings?"

Originally a livestock term for cutting unique marks into an animal's ear to prove ownership, earmarking evolved into setting aside specific funds for a dedicated purpose. In this app, an earmark reserves portions of your balance for designated goals or upcoming expenses, keeping that money protected from everyday spending.

Most budgeting apps report past expenses after you have already spent your money. Shear Budget is designed from the ground up for forward cash flow: it tracks your real bank balances, rings fences around your upcoming bills and earmarks, and calculates exactly how much safe "Fun Money" you can spend today without bouncing checks or eating into your rainy day stash.

Local-First Architecture & Security

Shear Budget is engineered from the ground up as a local-first financial tool. Unlike conventional cloud budgeting applications that centralize your most sensitive bank balances, transaction histories, and personal spending habits on third-party servers, Shear Budget prioritizes absolute privacy, offline autonomy, and cryptographic user ownership.

1. Your Device Is the Single Source of Truth

All of your accounts, transactions, earmarks, bills, and category rules are stored in a local encrypted database directly on your computer or phone (IndexedDB / SQLite). This brings immense benefits:

  • Full Offline Capability: You can review your budget, check your Available cushion, record cash expenses, fund earmarks, and adjust bills anywhere — even with no internet connection, in airplane mode, or during carrier outages.
  • Instantaneous Speed: Because your balance calculations and projections run entirely on-device, screens open immediately with zero loading spinners or network latency.
  • No Silent Data Mining: Your financial data is never collected, scanned, or sold to advertising brokers. If you use the Free tier, not a single transaction ever leaves your device.

2. Zero-Knowledge End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

When you synchronize between multiple devices (such as your desktop and mobile phone), your data remains completely confidential:

  • Client-Side Encryption: Data is encrypted directly on your device using industry-standard AES-GCM (256-bit) cryptography with a dedicated, cryptographically generated encryption key before leaving your device.
  • Zero-Knowledge Server: The sync server only stores opaque, encrypted binary blobs. Shear Budget engineers, server administrators, and potential cloud intruders cannot decrypt, inspect, or reconstruct your financial records.

3. Your Encryption Key & Recovery Key Code

Your encryption key is not tied to your account password—it is an independent cryptographic key that belongs exclusively to your budget. This distinction is vital:

Encryption Key Security Warning

Account passwords can be reset, but encryption keys cannot. If you forget your Shear Budget account password, you can reset it via email. However, because Shear Budget never has access to your encryption key, nobody can reset, recover, or bypass a lost encryption key.

When setting up a budget or multi-device sync, save your generated Recovery Key Code in a secure password manager or physical vault. To open or restore your encrypted budget on a new phone or computer, you will enter this key code. Without your Recovery Key Code, your encrypted financial data cannot be recovered by anyone.

4. Bank Feeds & Plaid Privacy

When using bank connectivity on the Plus tier:

  • Read-Only Tokenization: Shear Budget uses Plaid's official bank-grade APIs solely in read-only mode to retrieve transaction entries and posted balances. Shear Budget can never move money, initiate transfers, or make withdrawals.
  • No Stored Credentials: Your online banking username and password are exchanged securely through Plaid's direct institutional authentication flow and are never transmitted to, viewed by, or saved on Shear Budget's devices or servers.
  • Disconnect at Any Time: You can revoke bank connections instantly in Settings > Bank Connections or switch back to file import whenever you prefer.

5. Total Data Ownership & Portability

You are never locked in. Under Settings > Data Management, you can download a full unencrypted JSON backup of your entire budget history or export your transactions to CSV at any time. If you ever choose to leave, the Danger Zone provides a one-click irreversible wipe that purges all local tables, caches, and sync credentials from your device.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

  1. Step 1: Name Your Budget & Generate Your Encryption Key
    Before configuring accounts or seeing the dashboard, Shear Budget initializes your private, encrypted local vault:
    1. Enter a Budget Name: Choose a name for your budget file (e.g. Personal Budget or Household).
    2. Generate Zero-Knowledge Encryption Key: Click Create new budget & generate key (or sign in with your email, password, and existing recovery code if opening a cloud budget).
    3. Safeguard Your Recovery Key Code: Shear Budget generates a secure zero-knowledge AES-GCM encryption key stored directly on your device and displays your unique Recovery Key Code. Copy and store this recovery key in your password manager or physical vault, check "I saved this recovery code somewhere safe", and click Continue.
    Zero-Knowledge Key Notice
    Your encryption key is stored only on your client device and is never sent to any server. Shear Budget account passwords can be reset via email, but encryption keys cannot be reset or recovered by anyone. Always record your Recovery Key Code.
  2. Step 2: Choose Your Starting Option (Figure 1)
    Once your budget name and encryption key are established, you will see the welcome screen on your empty dashboard offering guided and direct pathways:
    • Guided setup walkthrough: (Recommended) The app guides you step-by-step through establishing your real bank balances (with the option to import directly from your bank or enter by hand), scheduling your expected income, listing your recurring monthly expenses, setting up your earmarks, and corralling your stash so you immediately see your safe daily spending cushion (can be skipped at any time).
    • Add Account (manually): Enter your current checking account name and balance by hand. Perfect for complete offline privacy without connecting external services.
    • Import CSV / OFX / QFX: Download a recent statement file from your online bank and upload it. Shear Budget automatically reads the ending balance and creates the account for you.
    • Restore backup: Restore your budget and transaction history directly from a previously exported .json backup file.
    • Sign in to account: Sign in with your Shear Budget email and password to sync with the cloud and connect bank accounts (available with Shear Budget Plus).
  3. Step 3: Add Your Primary Checking Account (Figure 2)
    Give your account a recognizable name (e.g. Primary Checking) and enter the current posted balance from your bank app, or click Import from bank on Step 1 of the guided walkthrough to automatically populate your balance from a downloaded CSV, OFX, or QFX statement file.
    The Cushion Rule
    By default, only checking accounts are included in your Available spending cushion. Savings accounts, IRAs, investment portfolios, and credit card liabilities are automatically ignored from your primary cash pool so they do not artificially inflate the money you think you can spend today.
  4. Step 4: Schedule Your Expected Income
    Add your employer paycheck dates and expected net deposits. Whether you are paid weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, or once a month, Shear Budget projects your balance curve ahead and applies the Final Paycheck Rule to guarantee you don't overspend before your next deposit clears.
  5. Step 5: Enter Your Recurring Monthly Bills
    Add your fixed upcoming commitments for the current month: Rent/Mortgage, Utilities, Car Payment, Insurance, and Streaming Subscriptions. Set each bill's due date and amount. Shear Budget immediately fences off this exact sum from your Available cushion.
  6. Step 6: Set Up Your Earmarks (Spending Envelopes)
    Create Earmarks for variable essentials like Groceries, Dining Out, and Vehicle Maintenance. Shear Budget automatically water-fills these earmarks with your remaining cash so everyday envelopes are funded before fun money is spent.
  7. Step 7: Corral Your Stash (Optional)
    Corral an emergency buffer in Stash (money physically in checking that you refuse to touch). Corralling your stash is optional; it removes your buffer from your daily spending cushion so you never accidentally graze on it.
  8. Step 8: Monitor Your Daily Available Cushion (Figure 3)
    Your dashboard is now fully active! Every morning, glance at Available to Spend. As long as this figure remains positive and the 30-day chart stays safely above your Stash line, your bills, savings, and future paychecks are completely protected.

The Calculations Explained

Every number you see in Shear Budget is computed using deterministic cash-flow arithmetic. Here is the exact mathematical foundation behind your dashboard.

1. Available to Spend ("Fun Money" / Cushion)

This is the primary headline metric on your Dashboard. It represents the money that is truly safe to spend right now.

Available = Adjusted Bank Balance − Stash − Unpaid Bills − Remaining Earmarks + Expected Deposits + Included Paychecks
+
Adjusted Bank Balance: Posted checking balance + pending items + transactions dated after your balance record.
−
Stash: Money physically inside your account reserved for emergency or goals (not spent, but held out of Available).
−
Unpaid Bills: Outstanding bills due in the current month that have not yet cleared.
−
Remaining Earmarks: Budgeted envelope spending allocated for the month minus what you have spent so far.
+
Expected Deposits: Verified one-off income expected to arrive before the month ends.
+
Included Paychecks: Future paychecks in the month, excluding the month's final paycheck.
Why Credit Cards & Savings are excluded from the Available balance

By default, only checking accounts are flagged as include: true. Your credit cards represent debt (not liquid funds), and your savings account holds separate funds. Only the accounts you select in Settings > Bank Accounts contribute to your liquid Available funds.

2. The Final Paycheck Rule (Crucial Concept)

A common pitfall in budgeting software is inflating your end-of-month funds with your final paycheck, leaving you broke when the 1st of the next month arrives with rent and mortgage.

Why is the last paycheck of the month excluded from the current month's Available total?

The last paycheck of any month is designated to fund the next month!

If you get paid on October 30th, that paycheck is not extra pocket money for October 31st. You need that money to pay rent, utilities, and groceries on November 1st before your mid-November paycheck arrives. Shear Budget automatically transfers this paycheck into the projected opening balance of next month, preventing artificial end-of-month spending sprees.

3. Pre-Paycheck Dip Warning

You may occasionally see a warning banner at the top of your dashboard:

Next month drops below $0 before paycheck on [Date] (-$180.00 dip)

What it means: Shear Budget simulates your cash flow day-by-day into the next month. If your bills due between the 1st of next month and your first next-month paycheck exceed your projected rollover balance, you will experience a cash crunch before getting paid.

How to resolve it: Leave extra funds unspent in your current month's Available balance, take funds from Stash, or adjust your bill schedule.

4. Stash vs. Paddock Savings

Shear Budget distinguishes between two kinds of savings reservations:

  • Standard Stash ("Ongoing Reserve"): Money physically held inside your bank account for emergency reserves or long-term goals. It is subtracted directly from Available funds today so you do not spend it. If you set a monthly contribution, Shear Budget automatically adds funds on the 1st of each month until your goal is reached, then automatically stops.
  • Paddock Stash: Marked with a paddock/fence icon (). Paddock funds are fenced off exclusively to boost next month's opening position (e.g. quarterly insurance, to cure a pre-paycheck stash dip or cover rent on the 1st). Like other Stash, Paddock's reduce your current month's Available funds.

5. Water-Filling Earmark Spread Algorithm

When you have unused earmarks (e.g., $300 left for groceries this month), how are they represented on your 30-day projection chart?

Shear Budget uses an intelligent multi-pass water-filling algorithm:

  • Instead of dividing your remaining earmarks equally across all 30 days (which could artificially make your balance dip below $0 right before a paycheck), it calculates the headroom available each day.
  • Days before a paycheck only absorb the earmark spending they can afford without dipping into your Stash.
  • Once your paycheck arrives, the remaining days absorb the rest of the planned earmarks evenly. This ensures your daily projection chart reflects realistic, safe spending.

2. Ear Tags & Stash Dips (Red vs. Yellow Explained)

Why does Shear Budget use livestock ear tags? In ranching, ear tags uniquely identify individual animals that need close observation. In Shear Budget, ear tags flag specific days on your cash-flow calendar where your bank balance crosses into a risk state.

Yellow Ear Tag: Dips into Stash (Recovers)

What happened: On November 8th, a large unexpected car repair ($650) caused the checking balance to drop to $850, dipping $350 into the $1,200 Stash reserve.

Why it is Yellow: A scheduled paycheck of +$1,800 lands on November 15th before any subsequent bill is due. Because your account recovers above the Stash floor without risking an overdraft or an unpaid bill, Shear Budget flags it in yellow.

Action required: None urgently required. The yellow ear tag keeps you informed of the temporary dip while assuring you that upcoming obligations are safe.

Red Ear Tag: Dips into Stash (Unrecovered)

What happened: On November 23rd, an annual insurance bill ($1,750) drove the balance down to $600 ($600 into Stash), with electric utilities still due on Nov 27th.

Why it is Red: The balance remains below Stash through the end of the month. Even though an end-of-month paycheck lands on Nov 30th, the Final Paycheck Rule designates that paycheck to pay December 1st rent. It cannot be spent in November.

Action required: Immediate attention needed. You should transfer funds from an outside savings account, reduce discretionary earmarks, or slide an eligible non-autopay bill to avoid a deficit.

Other Ear Tags You May See:

  • Slid Bill Ear Tag (proj-bill-slide-icon): Placed on the date of a non-autopay bill with a grace period that has been slid forward to payday to protect your Stash.
  • Unfunded Earmarks Ear Tag (proj-unfunded-earmarks-icon): Placed at month end when planned earmark envelopes exceed available cash flow.
  • Stash Card Ear Tag Badge: The Stash card on your dashboard displays a yellow or red ear tag trigger if any day in the month dips into your Stash reserves.

3. Bill Sliding Algorithm (Grace Periods)

Have you ever had a bill due on the 3rd, but your paycheck arrives on the 5th, and the company offers a grace period until the 10th without late fees?

Shear Budget includes an automatic Bill Sliding engine:

  • If a bill has a lateDate (e.g. 10th) and is not marked as Autopay, Shear Budget checks if paying it on the 3rd would dip your account into Stash.
  • If a dip would occur, Shear Budget slides the bill to your payday (the 5th) on the projection chart, showing you that you can safely wait for your paycheck to arrive before paying the bill.
  • Autopay bills never slide, because your bank will automatically process them on the exact due date.

4. Other Spending & Earmarks Donut Charts

Shear Budget displays two specialized donut charts:

  • Earmarks Donut: Displays your planned budget categories (Groceries, Dining, Fuel, Entertainment) showing the proportion of planned funds spent vs. remaining.
  • Other Spending Donut: Highlights transactions that do not belong to a bill or an earmark. This reveals hidden leaks in your budget that are eroding your Available Fun Money. Slices representing less than 3% of spending are automatically grouped into "Other" to keep the visual representation clean.

5. Account Balance & Debt Trends

In the Accounts view, Shear Budget projects long-term trends for up to 12 months ahead:

  • Checking & Savings Trend: Models your cash growth by compounding your monthly income minus recurring expenses and planned additions.
  • Debt Paydown Trend: Projects credit card and loan balances downwards based on your monthly payments towards principal, capping at $0 when fully paid off.

Step-by-Step "How-To" Guides

1. Bank Accounts & Statement Import

You can manage accounts manually, import statements, or connect your bank automatically via Plaid (available on Shear Budget Plus, supporting up to 5 bank institutions with multiple accounts each).

How to Import Bank CSV, OFX, or QFX Files:

  1. Go to Settings > Bank accounts or click the Import icon () on the Transactions toolbar.
  2. Under Put transactions on this account, select your target account.
  3. Click Choose File and select your downloaded .csv, .ofx, or .qfx file.
  4. If importing CSV for the first time, map the columns for Date, Amount, and Description. Shear Budget automatically remembers your column mappings for future imports.
  5. Click Import. Shear Budget automatically applies your categorization rules to new transactions and skips any duplicates.
Free Tier Account Limit Handling

The Free tier includes up to 3 bank accounts. If you are at your 3-account limit and import a file from a different bank, Shear Budget will prompt you to choose which existing account to replace with the imported data.

3. Earmarks (Envelope Spending)

Earmarks represent discretionary spending envelopes for categories like Groceries, Dining Out, Fuel, and Hobbies.

  • Monthly Budget: Set your target monthly allowance for the category.
  • Overage Protection: When you spend more than planned in an earmark, Shear Budget highlights the excess in red and deducts the overage directly from your Available Fun Money.
  • Create Earmarks & Paddocks: Click the Add earmark icon () to create standard envelopes, or Add Paddock () to set aside funds exclusively for next month.
  • Fund Earmark / Rebalance: Open any earmark's menu to select "Fund earmark" or "Take from another earmark" to move funds between envelopes when priorities change.

4. Bills & Recurring Obligations

Bills represent fixed, recurring obligations (Rent, Electric, Phone, Car Insurance, Netflix).

  • Recurrence Rules: Supports Monthly, Bi-weekly, Weekly, Semi-monthly, and Annual schedules.
  • Autopay Flag: Mark bills that automatically draft from your checking account. Autopay bills stay locked to their scheduled date.
  • Grace Period / Late Date: Set the final date a bill can be paid without penalties. Non-autopay bills can slide up to this date on the projection chart if cash is tight before payday.
  • Payment Linking: When your bank transaction posts, link it to the bill. The bill marks as Paid and will not be deducted twice.

5. Categories & Auto-Categorization Rules

Located in Settings > Categories and rules.

  • Matching Modes:
    • Contains: Matches any description containing the text (e.g. netflix).
    • Regex: Advanced regular expression matching (e.g. amazon|amzn|prime).
    • Wildcard: Use * and ? patterns (e.g. CHEVRON*).
  • Execution Order: Rules execute sequentially from top to bottom. The first matching rule assigns the category.
  • Apply to Existing: Click "Apply to existing" to run your rules across your historical transactions.
