Stash it. Earmark it. Know you’re covered.

Your bank shows your balance. Shear Budget shows what’s actually yours to spend.

Originally a livestock term for cutting unique marks into an animal's ear to prove ownership, earmarking evolved into setting aside specific funds for a dedicated purpose. In this app, an earmark reserves portions of your balance for designated goals or upcoming expenses, keeping that money protected from everyday spending.

Shear Budget starts from your posted bank balance, subtracts your Stash (set aside), Bills and Earmarks (planned spending) then shows if you have a cushion. That’s your "play money" and that's the difference. It is a local-first app for Windows desktop and Android. It works fully offline or signed out. An account is optional.

Questions

How much does it cost?

It's completely free! Shear Budget works entirely offline. You can build a budget using numbers only to see how earmarked funds affect your plans, or import bank transactions manually to track your spending in detail.

Do I need an account?

No. Shear Budget works fully offline or signed out. An account is optional.

What does the account do?

It syncs your budget between your phone and your desktop. The account can be set up after a low one-time purchase of the app. Sync is backed by Supabase.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Delete it in the app, or on the web at the account page (shearbudget.com/account). That deletes the login and the budget stored in the cloud.

How do transactions get in?

CSV import. You can split a transaction across categories, ignore a transaction, or let a rule categorize it. With the account and a small subscription fee, you can also import from your bank directly.

Will a bill be counted twice?

No. Transactions linked to bills do not double-count against earmarks. Bills and subscriptions are separate sections, each with a subtotal.

Can I change one month without changing every month?

Yes. Earmark edits can apply to this month only, or to all months. Earmarks can also roll over, and you can move between months.

Which devices?
