Privacy

Shear Budget is local-first. The app works fully offline or signed out. An account is optional.

An optional free account can sync a budget between a phone and a desktop computer. That sync is backed by Supabase.

You can delete a Shear Budget account in the app, or on the web at the account page. Deleting removes the login and the budget stored in the cloud.

This website does not use analytics or other trackers.

Shear Budget is made by Eagle Butte Software LLC, a software subsidiary of Eagle Butte LLC, based in Colorado. The company privacy page is at eaglesoftware.eaglebuttellc.com/privacy.
